01 · Who we areWho operates The Right Property Method™

The Right Property Method™ is an educational and real estate matching platform operated by DBX LLC, a company registered in the State of Florida, USA. In this Policy, "we", "the System" or "The Right Property Method" always refer to DBX LLC.

For any matter regarding personal data, the direct channel is contato@sistemaimovelcerto.com or phone +1 (305) 684-9224.

02 · DataWhat we collect about you

We only collect what is necessary to deliver the diagnostic. In order of timing:

A. During the quiz (before you provide email)

  • Your answers to the 5 questions (goal, lifestyle, ticket, household, decision timeline). They remain only in your browser until you submit the form.
  • Anonymous technical session data: device type, OS, browser, language, screen resolution, IP country — collected via Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate behavior.

B. When you request your result by email

  • Full name
  • Email
  • Phone number (with country code)
  • Full quiz profile (answers + 3 matched regions)

C. When you write or call us

  • Content of your message (email or phone)
  • Time and channel of contact
What we do NOT collect We do not collect SSN, driver's license, passport numbers, bank account data, credit card numbers, credit score, salary, tax returns, deeds to properties you already own, or any government-issued ID. Matching uses only psychographic profile — we don't require financial documents to deliver the diagnostic.

03 · UseWhy we use your data

  • Run the matching algorithm — cross-reference your answers with 305,000 Florida MLS listings and identify 3 compatible regions.
  • Send the result by email with regions, ticket ranges, and curated listings.
  • Enable Realtor partner contact (André Cunha Lima or Daniella) by phone within 1 hour — always with your full profile, so they don't ask you everything again.
  • Improve the platform via aggregate and anonymous analysis (Microsoft Clarity).
  • Track conversions on paid media, if you arrived via ad (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads).
  • Comply with legal obligations when US authorities require by court order.
What we NEVER do with your data We do not sell your email list. We do not exchange your data for affiliate lists. We do not share with developers, builders, banks, insurance carriers, or competing real estate portals.

04 · Legal basisLegal basis for processing

Under CCPA and applicable Florida state law:

  • Consent — when you submit the form requesting the diagnostic.
  • Contract performance — delivering the result and connecting the Realtor.
  • Legitimate interest — aggregate behavior analysis to improve the product.
  • Legal obligation — response to authorities when required.

05 · CookiesCookies, pixels, and tracking

We use the following tools, all with legitimate purpose and in compliance with applicable US privacy law:

  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymous session recording (no password or sensitive data capture) and heatmaps. Microsoft policy.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram Ads) — conversion tracking when active. Meta policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads — traffic metrics and conversions. Google policy.
  • TikTok Pixel — conversion tracking when active. TikTok policy.
  • Vercel (hosting) — technical server logs (IP, user agent) for up to 30 days. Vercel policy.

You can disable tracking cookies directly in your browser or with extensions like uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger. Essential diagnostic functionality continues to work.

We share your name, email, phone, and quiz profile exclusively with:

  • Formspree (processor) — handles form submission to our email. Formspree policy.
  • André Cunha Lima (FL-licensed Realtor®) or Daniella (partner Realtor®) — the only people authorized to contact you as transaction executor. André operates under a separate buyer representation agreement with you. We do not receive commission on that relationship (see Terms of Use).

We never sell your list. We never exchange data with third parties without contractual necessity.

07 · InternationalInternational data transfers

Since DBX LLC is headquartered in the US and uses US-based services (Formspree, Vercel, Microsoft, Google, Meta, TikTok), your data is stored and processed within the United States.

For users outside the US:

08 · RetentionHow long we keep your data

  • Quiz profile + name + email + phone: 36 months from your last contact with us. Renewed if you respond to any communication or re-engage with the platform.
  • Server logs (Vercel): 30 days.
  • Microsoft Clarity recordings: 13 months (Microsoft default).
  • Pixel tracking (Meta/GA4/TikTok): follows each platform's default retention.

After the term, data is irreversibly deleted or anonymized.

09 · Your rightsYour rights as a data subject (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you
  • Access a copy of the data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your data (we don't sell, but you can opt out anyway)
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

To exercise any of these rights, email contato@sistemaimovelcerto.com with subject "CCPA Request". We respond within 45 days as required by law.
